01 / Approach

The product is the fixed point.

Every production choice begins with a locked specification. Color, proportion, lapel, three-button closure, pocket construction and textile character are treated as evidence—not creative suggestions.

02 / Product consistency

One product.
Every angle accountable.

Front, back and rotational views are approved against the same locked specification. The three-button closure, short proportion and restrained construction remain fixed.

03 / Model application

Pose changes.
The product does not.

One adult model identity, natural skin texture and restrained styling across a complete commercial shot list.

04 / Production

AI-assisted.
Human-directed.

Automation supports controlled cleanup, composition and output adaptation. Human judgment defines what may change—and what must remain untouched.

QC / PASS

05 / Visual QC

Approval is a production decision.

A visually attractive output is not automatically usable. Every asset is compared against the product specification before it can move forward.

  1. 01 Color fidelity
  2. 02 Silhouette & proportion
  3. 03 Lapel, buttons & pockets
  4. 04 Fabric & stitching

Only verified assets move forward.

When the image is uncertain,
the status is uncertain.

No invented detail. No silent correction.

06 / Multi-format delivery

One approved source.
Five commercial contexts.

Reframing and cropping preserve source pixels. The product is never stretched to fit the channel.

07 / Outcome

Consistency over volume.

The final asset pool includes a verified product reference pack, one consistent seven-image model series, controlled retouching demonstrations and a channel-ready crop system. Rejected iterations remain outside the final selection.
